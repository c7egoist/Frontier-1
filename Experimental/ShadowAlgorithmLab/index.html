WHAT TO LOOK FOR

Why can AO look blocky at one distance?

Clipmap artifacts usually come from coarse world-space cells, a cascade switch, or too few rays—not from the car mesh changing. This benchmark locks its nested fields around the vehicle, so their pattern is stable as you dolly. A production camera-following clipmap can re-center or switch cascades as the view moves. Screen-space AO changes its world coverage with depth even when the scene is fixed.

Compare the same contact area, then vary fine voxel size. If blotches stay pinned to the surface and respond to voxel size, inspect UDF resolution/filtering; if they change with view distance or render scale, inspect screen-space radius and upscaling. Sudden changes can indicate a cascade transition.
